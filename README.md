# Tertis

## Local multiplayer

- Supports 2–4 players on the same Wi-Fi network or phone hotspot.
- Hosts are discovered automatically through Bonjour/NSD.
- The host accepts or declines every join request.
- Live compact opponent boards, scores, game-over state, and garbage lines are synchronized.
- The host can start Normal or Deadlock matches.
- Holding the lobby Deadlock button for 1.2 seconds reveals the hidden CHAOS option.
- Local Normal mode overrides each device's personal Deadlock setting so all players use the host's selected rules.

## Existing improvements included

- Hold Left, Right, or Down for continuous movement.
- Large player board with compact opponent previews.
- Button click sounds.
- Dedicated Standard, Deadlock, and CHAOS music.

## VS AI and CHAOS

- Easy, Normal, Hard, and hidden CHAOS AI difficulties.
- Repeatedly press Hard to reveal the hidden CHAOS difficulty.
- CHAOS forces Deadlock rules, disables the ghost preview, and enables freeze power-ups.
- The CHAOS instant-kill chance is rolled once per match. Only 1% of newly started CHAOS matches are armed; the game no longer rolls another 1% chance after every AI move.
- The player board stays large while the AI is shown as a compact live opponent board.
- Fixed AI Freeze occasionally trapping a newly spawned piece invisibly inside the hidden buffer. Frozen pieces are now revealed, and gravity resumes immediately when the three-second freeze expires.

## Gameplay and customization

- Standard 10×20 visible playfield with a hidden spawn buffer.
- Hold-piece swapping is limited to once per falling piece.
- Ghost landing preview can be enabled or disabled.
- Trick-fit rotations can award a timed bonus with visual feedback.
- Game speed increases with elapsed time and cleared-line level.
- Controls can be moved, resized, and made more transparent.
- Optional left- or right-handed compact control layouts.
- Pause-menu access to Options during a match.
- Username-based local high-score saving.
